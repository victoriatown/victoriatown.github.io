Cropped Image: 1920 x 1080

I chose this image because it captures the layered intensity of New York City, including its density and constant movement. I was drawn to the strong linear perspective created by the street running through the centre of the frame, which naturally guides the viewer’s eyes deep into the city. The surrounding buildings form a visual corridor, emphasizing scale and structure while framing the movement of cars and subtle street activity below. I used symmetry, leading lines, and depth to create visual interest, contrasting the rigid geometry of architecture with the organic flow of traffic. The cool tones and winter atmosphere further enhance the mood, reinforcing the feeling of observing the city from both within and above it.
