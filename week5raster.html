I chose this riverside environment because of its strong perspective and layered composition, which naturally divides the scene into foreground, middle ground and background. The cherry blossom trees frame the walkway and lead the eye toward the illuminated skyline, creating depth and movement. In transforming the photograph into an illustration, I simplified complex architectural and organic details into flat geometric forms using the Pen Tool, Shape Builder Tool and basic shape tools. Rather than recreating texture or fine detail, I focused on silhouette, repetition and colour blocking to reinterpret the environment in a more graphic and stylised way. Limiting the palette to roughly three colour made it difficult to adequately divide the different scenery components however I tried to create created cohesion and strengthen the contrast between spatial layers, to try create a structured, design-focused interpretation.
